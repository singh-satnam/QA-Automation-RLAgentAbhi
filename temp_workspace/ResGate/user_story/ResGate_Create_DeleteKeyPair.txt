1. Open RG login page 
2. Enter valid "PI" email & password
3. Click Sign In
4. PI lands on 'My Projects' page successfully
5. Click on hamburger menu and select 'Key Pairs'
6. Click on '+Create New' button and 'Create Key pair' dialog box appears
7. Click on 'Project' dropdown and select the second project
8. Fill 'Name' input box with a valid name as 'myauto'+ random number
9. Click on the 'File format' dropdown and select option with text 'pem'
9. Click on 'Create Key Pair'
10. When created key pair appears on 'Key Pairs' page, click on three dots
11. Click on 'Delete' button
12. Delete key pair modal appears - click on button 'Delete'
13. 'Deleted keypair successfully' message is displayed on top right
